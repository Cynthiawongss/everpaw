EverPaw

A home
that keeps.

For a pet you’ve lost, or one who lives here now. They eat, drink, nap and come when you call, in a room whose light follows your clock.
Nothing here goes hungry or lonely while you are away.
The home is free to keep, with or without buying anything.
EverPaw never sends “come back” messages.
